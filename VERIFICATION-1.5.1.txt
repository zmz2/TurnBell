Regression coverage added for 1.5.1
- Active turn watchdog alarms are restored after an ordinary service-worker
  start and sample the exact tab, route, completion ID, and route epoch.
- Failed notification creation stays pending and retries after the persisted
  backoff; a stable active notification is detected after worker restart and is
  neither recreated nor sounded again.
- Unlock does not repeat an active initial notification. If it disappears
  before or after unlock, TurnBell sends one generic replay; dismissing a locked
  notification acknowledges the queue.
- Repeated turn-start messages do not inflate the original assistant baseline.
- Delayed route-enter responses cannot replace a newer completion. A new turn
  started on a placeholder route moves after the conversation ID appears, while
  deliberate navigation to an existing conversation suspends it.
- A same-count regenerated reply can recover after A→B→A when its in-memory
  fingerprint changed and final-render evidence remains stable.
- Rapid route changes keep source DOM bound to its originating path, recover a
  pending destination completion after A→B→A, and allow an explicit new turn
  while old route nodes remain mounted.
- A stale-epoch final candidate stays queued for route recovery; route-enter
  storage failures retry, and an Instant completion can migrate when the user,
  answer, and permanent URL appear in one sample.
- An unfinished turn remains finalizable after a newer turn claims the same
  route; a delayed route move from an older document cannot reclaim it.
- Unlock replay claims the latest queue state after querying notification
  activity, and dismissal acknowledgement retries after transient local or
  session storage failures until both locked notification IDs are cleaned up.
- Updating an unrelated sync setting preserves an explicitly armed detector
  wait through its Instant completion.
- Notification retry state does not persist page titles, URLs, reply text, or
  fingerprints; Web Notification data does not contain a URL.
